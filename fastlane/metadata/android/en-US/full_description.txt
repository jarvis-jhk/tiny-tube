Tiny Tube is a small YouTube client for Android 14 and newer. Search for videos, subscribe to channels, and keep a quiet feed of new uploads. Play videos in a fullscreen landscape player with seeking, speed, brightness, and volume controls. Resume where you left off, or download videos for offline viewing. SponsorBlock segments can be skipped automatically.

The app starts with no subscriptions. Search for a video and long-press its plus button to subscribe to that channel. No account login is supported.

Tiny Tube uses YouTube's network service and SponsorBlock. It is an independent client, unaffiliated with YouTube or Google. Changes to YouTube may interrupt playback until the app is updated.
